XENAKIS PLUG-IN (VST3 + CLAP) FOR WINDOWS 11
============================================

1. Before unzipping: right-click the zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" (if you see it) > OK.
   That stops Windows from flagging the plug-in files as downloaded from the internet.

2. Copy the plug-in into the standard folders (Windows asks for permission once):

   VST3 (works in every DAW):
       copy the whole "Xenakis.vst3" folder into
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

   CLAP (Bitwig, Reaper, FL Studio 2024+ and other CLAP hosts):
       copy "Xenakis.clap" into
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP
       (create the CLAP folder if it isn't there)

   You only need one of the two. Installing both is fine; your DAW lists them separately.

3. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW.
   FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins.
   Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-ins > Rescan.
   Reaper: Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST/CLAP > Re-scan.

4. Load "Xenakis" as an effect on any mixer track or insert. Open it, drop in your stems,
   press PROCESS ALL, review, and drag samples straight from the list into your project.
   Chops you audition play through that track's output. "Preview level" (a plug-in
   parameter) sets how loud they are.

To update, close the DAW and replace the files with the new ones.
